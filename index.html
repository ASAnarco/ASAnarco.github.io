How German salary calculation works

Your Brutto salary is your gross employment income before deductions. Your Netto salary is what remains after wage tax and employee social-insurance contributions.

What this calculator considers

  • German tax class I–VI
  • 2026 wage-tax calculation
  • Child allowance factor
  • Church tax
  • Health-insurance additional contribution
  • Pension insurance
  • Unemployment insurance
  • Long-term care insurance
  • Saxony care-insurance rule

Why can the result differ from my payslip?

German payroll calculations contain many individual parameters. Your actual payslip can therefore differ depending on your exact insurance situation, allowances, employer payroll configuration and other circumstances.

The BMF publishes the official 2026 Programmablaufplan used for the wage-tax calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Brutto?
Brutto means your gross salary before deductions.
What is Netto?
Netto is the amount remaining after applicable deductions.
What is tax class I?
Tax class I is generally used for single employees without the conditions for another tax class.
Does church tax affect my net salary?
Yes. If you are liable for church tax, it can be deducted from your wage.
Is this an official government website?
No. GermanySalary is an independent website. The German Federal Ministry of Finance publishes the official wage-tax calculation program.
